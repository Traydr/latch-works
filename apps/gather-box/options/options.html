Shortcuts

On a supported page, use Right Shift + ] to toggle Gather Box and Right Shift + [ to start downloading.

Native Chrome shortcuts work regardless of whether the page or Gather Box has focus: toggle with Not assigned and download with Not assigned.

Change native shortcuts in chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
