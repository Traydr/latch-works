# Pane View browsing

After sign-in, Pane View presents your synced library as a folder tree and media grid. On your first visit in a browser it reopens the last folder you viewed.

![Pane View sign-in](/screenshots/pane-view/login.png)

## Sign in

Open your Pane View URL and enter the username and password configured for your library. Sessions last 7 days and renew while you keep using Pane View. **Sign out** is in the sidebar footer.

Failed sign-in usually means a wrong username or password, not a sync token issue. Sync tokens are for Lockstep only.

## Folder navigation

- Use the sidebar folder tree, the breadcrumb path in the header, or the header's **Parent**, **Prev folder**, and **Next folder** buttons.
- The grid shows thumbnails for images and videos in the current folder.
- `Cmd/Ctrl+B` shows or hides the sidebar.
- In windows at least 1024 px wide, the **detail panel** follows your selection. It shows a larger preview and metadata, with **Open Viewer**, previous/next, **Copy path**, **Download**, and **Delete**. Use **Show/Hide preview panel** in the header to toggle it.

## Open folders and media

- Click a folder entry to descend.
- On desktop, double-click or press Enter / F on media to open the viewer.
- On phones, a single tap opens folders, media, or comics. See [Mobile browsing](/docs/pane-view/mobile).
- Shift+W goes to the parent folder; Shift+S opens the focused folder; Shift+A and Shift+D move to the previous and next sibling folders.

## Floating toolbar

The toolbar at the bottom of the gallery holds:

- **Recursive:** flatten subfolders into one grid, useful for finding recent uploads across a creator tree.
- **Comic:** group image folders into comic entries (forces recursive), like Frame View's Comic control. Turning Comic off also turns Recursive off, and turning Recursive off leaves Comic mode.
- **Exclude:** leave chosen subfolders out of recursive and comic views (shown while either is on).
- **Sort** and **Shuffle**: see [Search and filters](/docs/pane-view/search-and-filters).
- **Refresh:** reload the current folder.

Recursive and Comic are unavailable at the archive root; open a folder first.

## Settings

**Settings** in the sidebar footer covers theme, thumbnail size, default recursive browsing, **Show images** / **Show videos**, **Remember PDF page and video position**, autoplay, looping videos, and looping viewer navigation. Settings are saved in this browser.

## Changing the remote library

Pane View never touches files on your disk and cannot upload. From the browser you can:

- **Delete** an item from the detail panel. It is hidden from the remote library, and the next Lockstep push restores it if the file is still in your archive.
- Use the **Manage** page (sidebar footer) to delete folders, purge deleted items, review sync history, or wipe the library.

Add or change media locally, then plan and push with Lockstep.
