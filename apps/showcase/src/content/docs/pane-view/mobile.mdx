# Mobile browsing

Pane View adapts the same gallery and viewer for phones and tablets.

## Phones

Below 768 px wide (iPhone and other phones):

- The folder sidebar opens as a sheet from the menu button.
- The path opens in its own sheet, and search opens from the search icon.
- A single tap opens folders, media, comics, or PDFs.
- The floating toolbar keeps recursive, comic, sort, and refresh reachable by thumb.

## iPad and tablets

At 768 px and wider, including iPad in both orientations:

- The sidebar stays docked and can be collapsed.
- A tap selects an item and a double tap opens it.
- The detail panel appears once the window is at least 1024 px wide.
- Keyboard shortcuts work with an attached keyboard, with the same keys as desktop.

## Touch navigation

- Use on-screen viewer controls (and Q/E with a keyboard) to move between items.
- Press and hold a playing video for a temporary 2× speed boost.
- There is no pull-to-refresh gesture. Use **Refresh** in the floating toolbar if cells look stale.

## Slow or blank thumbnails

Prefer Wi-Fi for the first load of large folders. Thumbnails are made the first time they are requested, and tiles that fail retry on their own, fetching a fresh signed link if needed.
