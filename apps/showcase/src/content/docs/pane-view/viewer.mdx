# Pane View viewer

The viewer covers images, GIFs, videos, PDF stories, and comic folders synced from your archive.

![Pane View fullscreen viewer](/screenshots/pane-view/viewer.png)

## Opening media

From the grid, double-click or press Enter / F. Escape closes the viewer or nested overlays.

## Still images and GIFs

Images scale to fit the viewport. Animated GIFs play in the viewer loop.

## Video

Use Space or **2** to play and pause. The viewer exposes standard controls for seek and volume where supported.

## Comics

Folders detected as sequential comics open in a page-turn reader. Use **Q** / **E** or arrows for previous and next pages — same muscle memory as Frame View.

## PDF stories

PDF entries open in an embedded reader with page navigation. Long works remember position within the session.

## Keyboard summary

| Keys | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Q / E | Previous / next item or page |
| Escape | Close viewer |
| Space / 2 | Play or pause video |
| ? | Shortcuts overlay |

See [Keyboard shortcuts](/docs/keyboard-shortcuts) for the full gallery and viewer table.
