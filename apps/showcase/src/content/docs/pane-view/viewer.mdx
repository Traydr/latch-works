# Pane View viewer

The viewer covers images, GIFs, videos, PDF stories, and comic folders synced from your archive.

![Pane View fullscreen viewer](/screenshots/pane-view/viewer.png)

## Opening media

From the grid, double-click or press Enter / F on desktop; tap on phones. Escape closes the viewer or nested overlays.

The viewer bar has **Copy path**, **Download**, a toggle between the preview and the original file, and fullscreen.

## Still images and GIFs

Images scale to fit the viewport. Animated GIFs play in the viewer.

## Video

| Control | Keys |
| --- | --- |
| Play / pause | Space or 2 |
| Seek backward 5s | 1 |
| Seek forward 5s | 3 |
| Temporary speed boost (2×) | Hold 4 (or press and hold on touch screens) |

The on-screen controls also include volume and a speed menu.

## Comics

With **Comic** mode on, image folders open in a vertically scrolling reader. **Q** / **E** or the arrow keys jump to the previous and next page.

## PDF stories

PDF entries open in an embedded reader that scrolls through the pages. **Q** / **E** move to the previous or next item, not the next page.

With **Remember PDF page and video position** turned on in Settings (it is off by default), Pane View saves where you stopped to your account, so it carries across browsers and devices.

## Keyboard summary

| Keys | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Q / E or Arrow Left / Right | Previous / next item (page in the comic reader) |
| Escape | Close viewer |
| Space / 2 | Play or pause video |
| 1 / 3 | Seek backward / forward 5s |
| Hold 4 | Temporary 2× speed boost |

Press **?** in the gallery to show the shortcuts overlay. See [Keyboard shortcuts](/docs/keyboard-shortcuts) for the full gallery and viewer table.
