# Oriel settings

Open Settings from the bottom of the sidebar or with `Cmd+,`. It opens at the section of the mode you were in, and Escape takes you back. The sections are **General**, **View**, **Tidy**, and **Sync**.

![Settings open at the View section](/screenshots/oriel/view-settings.png)

## General

- **Theme:** System, Light, or Dark. View, Tidy, and Sync all follow it.
- **Data folder:** where Oriel keeps thumbnails, Tidy's catalog and Holding, and Sync profiles, with **Show in Finder**.

Window size, position, and the display it was on restore on launch.

## View

**Gallery**

- **Remember last folder:** open it again when Oriel starts.
- **Enable recursive mode by default:** show the media in every subfolder too.
- **Thumbnail size:** 140 to 340 px wide.
- **Show images** / **Show videos:** what the grid displays.
- **Autoplay video on hover** and **Enable audio in video preview:** video tiles in the grid.

**Viewer**

- **Autoplay videos in viewer**, **Loop videos**, and **Loop viewer navigation**, which steps from the last item back to the first.

**Storage** shows the thumbnail cache and the media index, with **Clear thumbnail cache** and **Clear media index** buttons, and whether the bundled ffmpeg and ffprobe are available for video thumbnails. Clearing either removes derived data only, which rebuilds on the next scan. **Keys** opens the shortcut sheet, and **Diagnostics** has debug logging, performance monitoring, and a copyable report for a bug.

## Tidy

Preferred folders belong to the folder Tidy is on; the other Tidy settings apply to every folder.

- **Preferred folders:** Duplicates keeps the copy in these folders first. **P** on a version in the queue adds its folder.
- **Similar versions:** Strict, Balanced, or Loose, the same choice as Duplicates' **Matching** popover.
- **Spot variants:** keep pictures that differ in one area, like a face, an outfit, or a caption's language, and keep each picture's copy in the folder with the rest of its set.
- **Video quality:** Medium, High, or Very high, used when Convert re-encodes a video and for Shrink's AV1.
- **Encoders:** whether H.264, HEVC, AV1, AAC, AVIF, ffmpeg, ffprobe, and libvips are available in this build.
- **Background analysis:** find similar versions and measure soundtracks after each scan.

## Sync

Each profile is listed with its folder, server, and whether a token is saved, with **Edit…** on each and **Add profile** below. The **Tokens** row shows where they are kept: in the data folder, encrypted with a key that on macOS only your user account can read.
