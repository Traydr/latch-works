# Tidy

Tidy scans the folders you add and offers to put them right: duplicate copies, files to convert, junk, and videos that could be smaller. Nothing changes until you ask, and every change moves files to [Holding](/docs/oriel/holding-and-undo) rather than deleting them.

![Tidy's Overview page for a small archive](/screenshots/oriel/tidy-overview.png)

## Add a folder

Choose **Add folder…** (`Cmd+O`) from the location switcher at the top of the sidebar, or pick a folder View or Sync already use from **Tidy one you already use**. Tidy scans it and keeps a catalog in Oriel's data folder. Scanning never writes inside the folder, and the catalog can always be rebuilt by **Rescan** (`Cmd+R`).

After each scan, background analysis fingerprints every still and video to find similar versions and measures each soundtrack's loudness. It waits behind anything you ask for, and Settings can turn it off, after which Overview offers **Check now**.

## Overview

The top bar shows the folder's file count and size, **Open in View**, **Sync this folder**, and a rescan button. The card below adds up what tidying saves and has one button, **Tidy up** (`Cmd+Return`). Tidy up runs Clean up, silent-soundtrack removal, and Convert as one change with one Undo. It never resolves duplicates or shrinks video: those wait for your eye in their own pages.

Under the card, an **Also found** line names files that will not open, empty files, files no Latch Works app shows, files Pane View cannot resize (MKV and BMP), and files that cannot become MP4 (AVI, FLV, and ASF). Each opens the Library filtered to those files.

## Duplicates

Sets queue up in three scopes. **Identical** sets are byte-for-byte copies; you choose which folders keep one. **Similar** sets are versions that look alike, shown as a numbered grid where videos play silently. **Variants** are similar sets whose pictures each differ in one area, such as a face or a caption's language, so every version stays.

Tidy suggests a keeper and says why: a preferred folder, then sound, more pixels, a longer video, the set's home folder, the archive format (AVIF, or MP4 with H.264, HEVC, or AV1), a correct extension, a copy Tidy already shrank, a larger file, a name without a copy suffix, age, and path. Close calls are labelled as such.

- **Keep 1** (Return) keeps the suggestion. Press **1** to **9** to keep only that version, or Shift+**1** to **9** to keep it as well.
- **Keep both** / **Keep all** (N) marks the set as not duplicates. **Skip** (S) comes back to it later.
- **Space** opens the loupe to compare versions at actual size; **P** prefers a version's folder for the rest of the queue.

**Matching** in the top bar sets how alike two files must look: **Strict** for near-exact copies, **Balanced** (the default) for resized or recompressed copies, **Loose** for heavier compression and small edits, plus the **Spot variants** switch. Every decision shows a toast with **Undo**, and the copies you did not keep go to Holding.

## Convert

Convert brings files into the archive formats: **Pictures → AVIF** (PNG, JPEG, WebP, and BMP, at quality 70), **Animations → MP4** (GIF and animated PNG or WebP), and **Videos → MP4**. Video tracks already in H.264, HEVC, or AV1 are copied into the MP4 unchanged, others are re-encoded to H.264, and audio that is not AAC or MP3 becomes AAC. **Options** switches each of the three groups on or off, and **Convert N** (`Cmd+Return`) runs them.

A still or animation is converted only when the result is at least 5% smaller. Otherwise the original stays, Tidy says why under **left as they are**, and the file is not offered again until its bytes change. Converted files keep the original's modification date, so date sorting in View and Pane View holds. TIFF and HEIC stills are left as they are.

## Clean up

Clean up handles four kinds of mess at once or one card at a time: **silent soundtracks** (a peak below −60 dBFS) are removed from their videos, **junk files** such as `.DS_Store`, `Thumbs.db`, and `._*` resource forks go to Holding, **empty folders** (including folders holding only junk) are removed, and **wrong extensions** are put right, so an AVIF saved as `.jpg` becomes `.avif`. **Clean up N** (`Cmd+Return`) runs the lot.

## Shrink

Shrink re-encodes videos to AV1, biggest saving first, in runs of **5 GB**, **20 GB**, or **All**. Stop whenever you like: finished videos stay done, and their originals wait in Holding. As with Convert, a result must save at least 5% or the original stays. **Video quality** in Settings sets how hard AV1 compresses. Safari, and every browser on iPhone and iPad, plays AV1 only on A17 Pro, M3, and later chips, so Shrink is a choice, not part of Tidy up.

## Library

Library lists every file in the folder as a list or grid, with a search box (`/`), a **Filter** popover (kind, sound, format, issues, and folder), sortable columns, and an **Inspector** (I) with the file's path, format, dimensions, duration, sound, size, and issues. Select files to **Convert**, **Move to holding** (`Cmd+Delete`), **Move to…** (`Shift+Cmd+M`), or, from the **⋯** menu, **Quick Look**, **Open in View**, **Show in Finder**, **Rename** (Return), re-encode as HEVC, AV1, or H.264, remove an audio track, fix extensions, or prefer a folder for duplicates.

## After tidying

Tidy never talks to Pane View. To bring the web library in line, run Sync's **Plan**, then **Push**, then **Prune**: converted files show as uploads at their new paths and their originals as deletes. **Sync this folder** on Overview takes you straight there. Holding is outside every folder Sync reads, so held files never sync.
