# Privacy and safety

Latch Works is designed for a personal archive, not a public gallery. These practices keep control with you.

## Local archive authority

Files on your computer are yours. Frame View reads them locally. Nothing leaves your machine until Lockstep **Push** runs.

Treat your archive folder like any sensitive media directory: disk encryption and backups are your responsibility.

## Explicit sync

Lockstep never uploads in the background. **Plan** is always read-only. **Push** requires you to start it deliberately.

Review plan counts before pushing, especially after renaming or deleting folders. **Push** never applies deletes. Use **Prune** in Lockstep desktop or the CLI when you intend to remove remote entries that no longer exist locally.

## Authentication

Pane View requires sign-in. Credentials are separate from your sync token:

- **Sign-in** controls who can browse the web gallery.
- **Sync token** authorizes Lockstep to register uploads with your library.

Repeated failed sign-ins are throttled. Store sync tokens only in Lockstep (encrypted by the OS when available) or in an environment variable or secret store for CLI use. Do not share tokens in chat or email.

## Signed delivery links

Pane View thumbnails, previews, and originals use **signed links** that expire after about a day. Browsers load media from those temporary URLs instead of a permanent public path.

That means:

- Shared links stop working after expiry.
- Hotlinking your library from unrelated sites is not supported by design.

## Gather Box and site sessions

Some downloads use your browser login. The Gather Box cookie policy in Settings is **Auto (site defaults)**, **Always include cookies**, **Never include cookies**, or **Per-site overrides**. Tighten it if you want less cookie use.

Only collect from sites you are authorized to download from.

## Network exposure

Pane View should be reachable only over HTTPS on infrastructure you trust. Lockstep stores your Pane View API URL in the profile, so use the same host you intend to browse.

Run **Doctor** (desktop or CLI) after changing URLs or tokens to catch misconfiguration early.
