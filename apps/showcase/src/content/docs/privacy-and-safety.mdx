# Privacy and safety

Latch Works is designed for a personal archive, not a public gallery. These practices keep control with you.

## Local archive authority

Files on your computer are yours. Frame View reads them locally. Nothing leaves your machine until Lockstep **push** runs successfully.

Treat your archive folder like any sensitive media directory: disk encryption and backups are your responsibility.

## Explicit sync

Lockstep never uploads in the background. **Plan** is always read-only. **Push** requires you to start it deliberately.

Review plan counts before pushing, especially after renaming or deleting folders. **Push** never applies deletes — use **Apply deletes** in Lockstep desktop when you intend to remove remote entries from the plan.

## Authentication

Pane View requires sign-in. Credentials are separate from your sync token:

- **Sign-in** controls who can browse the web gallery.
- **Sync token** authorizes Lockstep to register uploads with your library.

Store sync tokens only in Lockstep (encrypted by the OS when available) or in a secure secret store for CLI use. Do not share tokens in chat or email.

## Signed delivery links

Pane View thumbnails and previews use **signed links** that expire. Browsers load media from those temporary URLs instead of a permanent public path.

That means:

- Shared links stop working after expiry.
- Hotlinking your library from unrelated sites is not supported by design.

## Gather Box and site sessions

Some story downloads use your browser login. Gather Box can send credentials with requests when allowed in extension settings. Use **per-site** or **never** modes if you want tighter control.

Only collect from sites you are authorized to download from.

## Network exposure

Pane View should be reachable only over HTTPS on infrastructure you trust. Lockstep stores your Pane View URL in the profile — use the same hostname you intend to browse.

Run **Doctor** after changing URLs or tokens to catch misconfiguration early.
