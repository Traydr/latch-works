# Gather Box

Gather Box is a Chrome extension that saves images, videos, and story PDFs from supported pages into folders on your computer. It is the **Gather** step in the Latch Works pipeline.

![Gather Box side panel ready to download](/screenshots/gather-box/popup.png)

## Role in the workflow

1. You browse a supported post, artwork, or work page.
2. Gather Box detects the page type and prepares a download list.
3. Files land in inferred folders beneath the destination you choose.
4. Frame View browses those folders locally; Lockstep syncs them when you push.

Gather Box does not upload anything to Pane View by itself.

Source metadata sidecars (`.latch-works.source.json`) are **planned but not shipped** — see
the [architecture sidecar design](https://github.com/Traydr/latch-works/blob/main/docs/gather-box-sidecar-manifests.md)
for the schema. Today only downloaded files land on disk.

## What it collects

- **Media** — images and (on some sites) video into site-specific folders.
- **Stories** — one PDF per work (site-generated or locally merged, depending on the source).

Supported sites are listed only in [Supported sources](/docs/supported-sources). Other pages appear as unsupported in the side panel.

## Before you collect

- Pick an archive root on disk and reuse it across sessions.
- Stay signed in on sites that require login for downloads.
- Confirm the side panel shows a supported page before starting.
