# Collect stories as PDF

Story collectors produce a **single PDF** per work — suitable for reading in Pane View after sync. Frame View PDF reading is planned, not shipped.

## Supported story sources

| Site | How the PDF is produced |
| --- | --- |
| Archive of Our Own | Downloads the site-generated PDF |
| FanFiction.Net | Fetches chapters and merges them locally into one PDF |

See [Supported sources](/docs/supported-sources) for the full site list (media vs stories).

## Steps

1. Open the work or story URL while signed in if the site requires it.
2. Open the Gather Box side panel and choose a destination folder (often your archive root or a stories subfolder).
3. Start the download and wait for completion.
4. Confirm the output file appears in the results log under **More**.

## Output naming

Story PDFs typically use `Author-Story.pdf` with spaces replaced by underscores. They save **directly into the chosen root** — Gather Box does not create a nested site folder for stories.

## Credentials and login

Story sites often need your browser session. Gather Box credential modes in Settings:

- **Auto** (default) — include cookies for sites that need them by default
- **Always** — include cookies for every download
- **Never** — never send cookies
- **Per site** — override per collector

Defaults with cookies on include pixiv and AO3. FanFiction.Net defaults off.

If a download returns login HTML instead of content, sign in on the site and retry.

## After download

Open the PDF in Pane View after sync to verify formatting. Large FanFiction.Net works may take longer to merge — wait for the side panel to report completion before closing the tab.
