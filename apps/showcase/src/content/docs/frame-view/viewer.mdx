# Viewer controls

The viewer opens images and videos over the gallery.

![Frame View media viewer](/screenshots/frame-view/viewer.png)

## Opening and closing

- Double-click a grid item or press Enter / F.
- Press Escape to close and return to the grid.
- The title bar has **Copy path**, **Reveal in folder** (shows the file in your OS file manager), **Fullscreen**, and **Close**.

## Image navigation

- **Q** or **Arrow Left**: previous item
- **E** or **Arrow Right**: next item
- On-screen arrows at the sides do the same.

**Loop viewer navigation** (on by default) wraps previous/next at the ends of the list. Sort order and recursive settings still apply to what is in the list.

## Video playback

| Control | Keys |
| --- | --- |
| Play / pause | Space or 2 (or click the video) |
| Seek backward 5s | 1 |
| Seek forward 5s | 3 |
| Temporary speed boost (2×) | Hold 4 (or press and hold the video) |
| Mute / unmute | M |

The on-screen bar also provides seek, volume, and a speed menu from 0.5× to 2×. Volume and mute are remembered between sessions.

## Fullscreen

Use the **Fullscreen** button in the viewer title bar or `View → Toggle Full Screen` in the app menu.

## GIFs and large images

Still images scale to fit the viewport. Very large files may take a moment to decode, so wait before skipping to the next item if the screen is blank.
